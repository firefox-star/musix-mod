================================================
 MUSIX ASSIST v1.2 - SETUP GUIDE (standalone)
================================================

WHAT THIS APP IS
- A tiny helper (25 KB) that gives you a floating orb icon
  and protects MusiX from being killed by the system.
- It does NOT contain MusiX. Your original MusiX app and
  all your songs stay exactly where they are.

STEP 0 - CLEAN UP THE OLD BROKEN THINGS (one time)
1. Uninstall "MusiX Mod" (the copy that closes instantly).
2. Uninstall "MusiX Assist" if it is still on the phone
   (the old crashing one). Note: MusiX Mod and the old
   MusiX Assist are the SAME app - uninstalling removes
   both icons together.
   *** DO NOT touch the original green "MusiX" icon ***
   *** that has all your songs. Keep it. ***
3. In your file manager / Downloads, DELETE the old file
   "MusiX-v4.0.5-KeepAlive-Assist-Mod.apk". It is obsolete
   and can never install - that is why it showed "Update".

STEP 1 - DOWNLOAD
Open this link in your browser:
https://github.com/firefox-star/musix-mod/releases/download/v1.2/MusiX-Assist-v1.2.apk
The file is "MusiX-Assist-v1.2.apk" (about 25 KB).

STEP 2 - INSTALL
1. Tap the downloaded file -> Install.
2. If Play Protect shows a warning: tap "More details"
   then "Install anyway". It only warns because the app
   is not from the Play Store.

STEP 3 - FIRST RUN (2 minutes, do once)
Open "MusiX Assist" and do these in order:
1. "Grant overlay permission" -> find MusiX Assist -> Allow.
   (This is what makes the floating orb appear.)
2. "Add to battery whitelist" -> Allow.
3. "Open original MusiX App Info" -> Battery -> Unrestricted.
4. "Open Autostart / Background rules" -> enable BOTH
   "MusiX" and "MusiX Assist" in the list.
5. Recents screen -> long-press both app cards -> Lock
   (the padlock).
6. Reboot the phone once. Done!

HOW TO USE THE FLOATING ORB
- A small round orb floats on your screen (over any app).
- DRAG it anywhere you like - the position is remembered.
- TAP it -> a menu opens with:
    "Open MusiX"  - opens your original MusiX instantly
    "Hide orb"    - removes the orb from the screen
    "Quit Assist" - stops the helper completely
- Want the orb back later? Open "MusiX Assist" and turn ON
  "Floating orb icon on screen".

WHAT IF...
- "MusiX Assist keeps stopping"? It will not happen with
  v1.2 - the watchdog revives the helper silently, and
  every risky call is wrapped so it can never throw an
  error dialog.
- The orb disappeared? Open "MusiX Assist" once, the orb
  returns (the watchdog also brings it back on its own).
- You want to remove everything? Just uninstall
  "MusiX Assist". Your original MusiX and all songs are
  never touched by installing or removing this helper.
