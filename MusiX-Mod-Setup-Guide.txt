----------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 0 - CLEANUP (2 minutes, IMPORTANT)
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. UNINSTALL the old broken "MusiX Mod" (the one that opened and
   closed instantly). Its icon disappears. That is normal.
2. DELETE every old MusiX APK you downloaded before (the Downloads
   folder: MusiX-Mod-v1.1-Standalone.apk, the very first keep-alive
   APK, etc.). Old files cause the confusing "Update" button.
3. DO NOT touch the ORIGINAL MusiX (green icon with your songs).
   Leave it exactly as it is.

 STEP 2 - INSTALL
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Tap the downloaded APK.
2. If Play Protect warns: tap "More details" -> "Install anyway".
   (Normal for any re-signed app outside the Play Store.)
3. Tap Open.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 STEP 3 - FIRST RUN (the setup panel opens BY ITSELF)
----------------------------------------------------------------
On first launch, "MusiX Assist" (the mod's control panel) opens
automatically on top. Do its checklist once:

 1. "Grant overlay permission" -> Allow  (this shows the orb)
 2. "Add to battery whitelist" -> Allow
 3. "Open MusiX App Info" -> Battery -> Unrestricted
 4. "Open Autostart / Background rules" -> allow MusiX Mod
    (and the original MusiX too, if it is listed)
 5. Recents screen: long-press both cards -> Lock (padlock)
 6. HiOS / Phone Master battery: turn OFF "Deep optimization"
    and "Auto-kill background"
 7. Reboot the phone once.

Then close the panel (back button) and USE MusiX Mod like a normal
music app: grant audio permission when asked, pick your songs.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 THE FLOATING ORB
----------------------------------------------------------------
- Appears once overlay permission is granted and the app runs.
- Drag it anywhere; tap it -> Open MusiX / Hide orb / Quit.
- The orb belongs to MusiX Mod and opens MusiX Mod.
- If the system ever kills the mod, the watchdog quietly revives
  it - no error pop-ups, ever.

----------------------------------------------------------------
 FAQ
----------------------------------------------------------------
Q: Will it delete my songs from the original MusiX?
A: No. Songs live in shared storage. Both apps read the same
   files. The original app is never modified.

Q: Two MusiX apps on the phone now?
A: Yes - the green ORIGINAL (untouched, your songs) and "MusiX
   Mod" v2.0. Different package names, like WhatsApp + GBWhatsApp.

Q: I still see "Update" instead of "Install"?
A: You opened an OLD downloaded APK file. Delete it and download
   v2.0 fresh (Step 1 link).

Q: The orb did not appear / I closed the setup panel too early?
A: Just open (or reopen) MusiX Mod - the setup panel pops up again
   automatically UNTIL overlay permission is granted. After it is
   granted the panel stops auto-opening (normal use).

Q: Can I uninstall the original MusiX now?
A: Your choice - but we recommend keeping it as backup. The mod
   does not depend on it in any way.
